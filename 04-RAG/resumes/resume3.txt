The story opens with Harry spending another difficult summer with the Dursleys. This time, things take a serious turn when he inflates his Aunt Marge after she insults his parents, causing Harry to run away from home.

Harry is picked up by the Knight Bus, a magical triple-decker bus that helps stranded witches and wizards. He stays at the Leaky Cauldron before heading back to Hogwarts.

At Hogwarts, Harry learns that Sirius Black, a notorious prisoner from Azkaban prison, has escaped and is believed to be after him.

The wizarding world is on high alert because Sirius was thought to have betrayed Harry’s parents to Voldemort, which led to their deaths.

Hogwarts is protected by Dementors, dark, soul-sucking guards from Azkaban, who patrol the school grounds looking for Sirius Black.

Harry finds Dementors especially terrifying, causing him to faint during their first encounter. He later learns to defend himself against them using the Patronus Charm.

Harry reconnects with his best friends Ron and Hermione. They face new challenges, including the arrival of the strict new Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher, Professor Lupin.

Lupin quickly becomes a favorite teacher, helping Harry learn the Patronus Charm to repel Dementors.

Throughout the year, Harry and his friends deal with typical school challenges, Quidditch matches, and exams, but the threat of Sirius Black looms large.

Harry also learns more about his parents’ past and their friendship with Sirius Black and another man, Peter Pettigrew.

The mystery deepens when Harry, Ron, and Hermione discover that Pettigrew, long thought dead, is actually alive and hiding as Ron’s pet rat, Scabbers.

Sirius Black is revealed to be innocent of the betrayal he was accused of; it was Pettigrew who betrayed the Potters.

The trio confronts Pettigrew, but he escapes, and Sirius is still in danger because the Ministry wants to capture him.

Harry and Hermione use a magical device called a Time-Turner to go back in time and save both Sirius and a hippogriff named Buckbeak from unjust execution.

Sirius escapes on Buckbeak, finally free from the unjust imprisonment that kept him isolated for years.

The school year ends with the truth about Sirius known only to Harry and his closest friends. Harry gains a new godfather figure in Sirius, who promises to look after him.

The story explores themes of friendship, loyalty, and the complexity of good and evil.

Harry returns to the Dursleys for the summer, hopeful but aware of the challenges ahead.

The novel deepens the lore of the wizarding world, especially with new magical creatures like Dementors and new spells.

It sets up future conflicts while providing a thrilling, emotional journey of growth for Harry and his friends.
