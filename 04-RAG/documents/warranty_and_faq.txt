STANDARD VEHICLE WARRANTY

The new vehicle warranty runs for 3 years with no distance limit. It covers defects in materials and
workmanship. It does not cover wear items, damage from misuse or accident, or the consequences of
maintenance that was not carried out.

Wear items excluded from the warranty include brake pads and discs, wiper blades, tyres, clutch friction
material and light bulbs. These are expected to be replaced during normal ownership.

HIGH VOLTAGE BATTERY WARRANTY

The high-voltage battery has a separate and longer warranty of 8 years or 160,000 kilometres, whichever
occurs first.

The battery warranty covers capacity retention as well as outright failure. If usable capacity falls
below 70 percent of the original value within the warranty period, the battery is repaired or replaced.
Gradual capacity loss above that threshold is considered normal ageing and is not a defect.

Capacity is assessed using the manufacturer's diagnostic procedure. Estimates shown by third party
applications are not accepted as evidence, because they infer capacity indirectly and vary widely.

CORROSION AND PAINT

Protection against rust perforation from the inside outward runs for 12 years. Paint surface defects are
covered for 3 years. Corrosion caused by unrepaired stone chips or accident damage is not covered.

WHAT CAN INVALIDATE COVER

Cover can be refused where required maintenance was not performed or not documented, where the vehicle
was modified in a way that affects the failed component, where the vehicle was used in motorsport, or
where the odometer has been tampered with.

Servicing at an independent garage does not by itself invalidate the warranty, provided the correct
specification parts and fluids were used and the work is documented.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Does fast charging damage the battery? Frequent DC fast charging accelerates ageing compared with AC
charging, but occasional use on long journeys has limited effect. Charge on AC for daily use and use DC
when travelling.

Should I charge to 100 percent? Not routinely. Charging to 80 percent for daily use reduces the time the
pack spends at high state of charge, which is the main controllable ageing factor. Charge fully when the
range is genuinely needed.

Why is my winter range lower? Two reasons. Cabin heating consumes energy that does not move the vehicle,
and cold cells are less efficient and briefly limit regeneration until warmed. A drop of 20 to 30 percent
in deep cold is normal.

Why did my charge take longer than expected? Charging speed depends on state of charge, battery
temperature and how the charging station shares power between vehicles. A cold pack or a shared charging
post will both extend the session.

Is the warranty transferable? Yes. Remaining cover transfers with the vehicle to a new owner at no cost,
which supports resale value.

Do software updates cost anything? Updates that correct faults or maintain compliance are free. Optional
feature upgrades purchased after delivery are chargeable.
